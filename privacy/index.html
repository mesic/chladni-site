Privacy Policy

Effective October 1, 2026

In short: Chladni does not collect, store or share any personal data. Everything happens on your Mac.

Who is responsible

Chladni is developed by Almir Mesic, Norway. Contact: course@fsbitesized.com.

Audio

To visualize sound, Chladni captures the audio your Mac is playing, using Apple's system audio capture. macOS asks for your permission the first time, and you can withdraw it at any time in System Settings → Privacy & Security.

  • The audio is analyzed in memory, in real time, only to draw the visuals.
  • The audio is never recorded, saved to disk or sent anywhere.
  • Chladni does not use the microphone.

Data we collect

None. Chladni has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party services, and it does not connect to the internet.

Data stored on your Mac

Chladni saves a small preference on your Mac: which displays to use in background mode. It stays on your Mac, is never sent anywhere, and is removed when you delete the app.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. Chladni never sees your payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies to those transactions.

Children

Chladni is suitable for all ages and does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date.

Contact
